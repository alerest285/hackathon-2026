# 2026-10-17 17:45: the SO-101 Hanoi stack is on main and safety-reviewed; zero-shot plans are still out of distribution

alerest285 / claude-code-hanoi (T06, T07, T10, T11).

## TL;DR

The server, the Mac client and the Hanoi runner are on main and tested. They have been through an adversarial
review and Codex's safety audit, and the server runs on the Runpod H100. The first live zero-shot run (Saad, 15:16Z)
executed its commands well: tracking p50 0.7°, p95 3.8°. The plans never went toward the cube, and consecutive plans
disagreed. The suspects are the joint convention and the scene, and T24 checks them before the next live run.
Nothing on the Hanoi path has run on hardware yet.

## What works

- **Server (T10), `infra/gpu/so101/`.** It speaks so101.v1 with `flux-action`, `lerobot` (+ `--adapter`) and `fake`
  engines. On the H100 a plan takes p50 514 ms eager and about 351 ms with `--compile` at the same sampling (Euler 4,
  guidance 3/3), using 15.8 GiB. A new caption's first plan takes about 5 s after the first compile. 21 tests pass.
- **Client (T06), `robot/`.**
  - The 30 Hz loop runs with prefetch, delta re-anchoring and a 6-tick crossfade, and skips the ticks the arm
    already moved through after a starvation hold.
  - The safety layers of decision 10: the loop clamp; a `NativeGuard` in `SO101.write` that raises `SafetyStop` on
    |goal − measured| > 25°, a jump > 15° or a goal outside the native limits; and latched stops.
  - History is factual: a failed write never becomes history, and a dry run keeps the held command.
  - Codex verified 3fb5132 and f00bff8. 63 tests pass.
- **Hanoi runner (T07), `eval/`.**
  - Decision 05's protocol: strict and recover runs, sanity runs and demo mode.
  - A step ends on the operator's key, on the grasp → release → rest detector (closing on air counts as a miss),
    or on a timeout. An infrastructure fault ends the attempt as `error` and stays out of the totals.
  - The real arm runs behind console's `MappedRobot` and the native guard. `--live` refuses to start without native
    limits, without the map, or with a map fitted for another robot id or calibration.
  - Every attempt is also one LeRobotDataset episode in `~/hackathon-data/so100_hanoi_eval`, in native units.
    decision 09's `train/prepare.py` reads its sidecars.
  - 74 tests pass, among them a 7-step episode over a real socket against `server.py --engine fake`, and a mapped
    fake episode whose 7 dataset episodes are read back with `LeRobotDataset`.
- **Docs.** architecture.md's SO-101 section matches the code. Decision 10 sets the units and safety contract,
  which decision 09 (training) follows.

## What is broken or unknown

- **Zero-shot plans are out of distribution.** The map (T21, experimental) has two hand-checked joints. The scene
  camera looks down from the far side, unlike the training corpus. The instruction said "box" for a round tray.
  Record: `docs/research/2026-10-17-saad-live-run-1.md`; post-mortem `docs/runs/2026-10-17-zero-shot-run-1.md`
  (claude-code).
- **Latency on Saad's link.** Turnaround was 745 ms p50 eager, and claude-code expects about 550–600 ms with
  `--compile`. With one request in flight, prefetch is stall-free up to about 700 ms. That passes now, but the margin
  is thin.
- **Untested on hardware:** the Hanoi runner, the auto-end thresholds (`closed 15 / open 25 / empty 3` are guesses
  until `robot/readout.py`), the adapter serving path (no checkpoint yet), and the 25/15/2° guard thresholds under
  real motion.
- The physical Hanoi set (pieces, pegs, names in `eval/config/hanoi.toml`) is not chosen yet.
