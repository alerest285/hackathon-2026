## What works

- **Adapter serving** (`--engine lerobot --adapter <ckpt>/pretrained_model`).
  - CPU: raw and EMA, eager and `--compile`; all 153 tensors equal LeRobot's `make_policy` PEFT path.
  - H100: `train.sh smoke` loaded raw and EMA through the server (PASS).
  - The base and the normalization come with the adapter. The startup line prints the served
    `state_q01/q99` and `action_q01/q99`.
- **`--norm-stats <json>`** gives zero-shot with any normalization without a checkpoint copy. Both engines produce
  bit-equal plans under the same override. With `--adapter` it only checks the stats and refuses a mismatch.
- **`eval/run_task.py`** scores what the grading counts: attempts, successes, completed steps and the longest chain.
  - Keys: enter starts an attempt, `+` per object on its side, `-` undoes one, space pauses (an intervention),
    `s`/`f` ends it, `q` quits. A timeout leads to a verdict prompt.
  - Defaults in `eval/config/task.toml`: Saad's caption, cameras 2/4, `my_follower_arm`, no joint map
    (HF-sim takes native degrees).
  - Each attempt is also one LeRobot episode, with its steps in the sidecar.
  - `python -m hanoi_eval.summary --status eval/runs/*-task` prints the comparison table.
  - 81 eval tests pass.
- **Recorders are safe against one-frame episodes.** LeRobot 0.6.1 cannot finalize a dataset holding a 1-frame
  episode, so eval and console now discard attempts under 10 frames.
